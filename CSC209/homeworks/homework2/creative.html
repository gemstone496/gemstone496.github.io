From Ashes May She Rise

From Ashes May She Rise is [will be] a [web]comic
written and drawn by Jade Onyx Violet Lilian.
If I ever get around to drawing more of her.

The story begins with a girl burning her home down, and
follows her once she gets taken in by an older trans
woman, and following her as she learns both to live
again after the trauma she has done but also to reconcile
the harm and destruction she has caused with the life
she now lives.

The first two pages are still works-in-progress, and
the rest has yet to be drawn, although some pieces
have been scripted. Obviously, this won't be a real
project for years, but when I do have time to draw
it, I'd like to be able to build myself a website for it.

Creative: 150 mins
